01 / The game

Small scale.
Big decisions.

From your first workers to the last enemy building: every decision changes the battlefield. Manage your base, prepare your units and find your moment to advance.

  1. 01

    Grow your economy.

    Assign workers to gather minerals and gas. A good strategy starts long before the first battle.

  2. 02

    Build your plan.

    Construct buildings, produce units and research upgrades. Turn your resources into an army ready for what comes next.

  3. 03

    Take command.

    Move your troops, coordinate their orders and destroy enemy buildings. Practice against AI or find a 1v1 duel.
