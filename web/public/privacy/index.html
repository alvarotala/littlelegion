Privacy policy

This policy explains how Little Legion uses information when you visit this website, play the game or contact support.

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Little Legion is responsible for the services described here. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email support@littlelegion.app.

2. Information we use

  • Online play: a random identifier generated by the game and stored on your device is used to authenticate your account. The service uses account and session IDs, matchmaking searches, your chosen faction, game versions, match results and ranking scores.
  • Connections: servers receive your IP address and technical data needed to process requests and connections. Operational logs may include timestamps, errors and connection activity.
  • On your device: the game may store preferences, match history and replays. This local data is not automatically sent to support.
  • Support: we receive your email address, message content and any files you choose to attach.
  • Website: the server processes the technical data needed to deliver these pages. This website does not include advertising cookies, analytics tools or data collection forms.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide online play, authenticate sessions, find opponents, manage matches and rankings, fix errors, protect the service and respond to requests. The authentication identifier generated by the game is not your device serial number.

4. Sharing information

Hosting and email providers process information to deliver their services. Other players may see information needed for a match and data displayed in the rankings. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

5. Retention and security

We retain data as needed to operate accounts, matches, rankings and support, or meet legal and security obligations. We review deletion requests to remove or anonymize the relevant data; backup copies may remain temporarily until they are rotated. We apply security measures, although no system can guarantee complete protection.

6. Your choices and rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data by emailing support@littlelegion.app. Describe your request and include any available public player ID. We may ask for proportionate additional information to verify ownership. Do not send passwords, tokens or authentication keys.

Deleting the app or its preferences may remove local data and the identifier needed to access your account again, but does not by itself delete server records. Contact support for those records. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights and the ability to contact a competent data protection authority.

7. Children and external platforms

Use of the game must comply with the age and account requirements of the platform you use. If you believe a child has shared information that should be deleted, contact us. Platform services, stores and email providers have their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and indicate the revision date. Where appropriate, material changes will be communicated through available channels.
