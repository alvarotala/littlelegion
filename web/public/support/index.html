We’re here to help.

A question, a problem or a good idea. Get in touch.

GET IN TOUCHsupport@littlelegion.app ↗

We handle support and privacy requests in English and Spanish.

Tell us what happened

To help us reproduce a problem, include your device model, game version, what you were doing and steps to reproduce it. If possible, attach a screenshot or a short video.

Do not send passwords, session tokens or payment information. Before sharing logs, check them for personal information.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Start with the guided tutorial and practice against AI to learn how to gather resources, build your base and command units.

What if I have connection problems?

Check your connection and make sure you and your opponent are using the same game version. Restart the game and try again. If the problem persists, email us with the error message and the approximate time of the incident, including your time zone.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Email support@littlelegion.app with the subject “Data deletion request”. Tell us which data you want deleted and include any available player ID. We will help identify your account and verify your request. Do not send your authentication identifier or session tokens.

Where can I learn about how my data is used?

Our privacy policy explains what information is used, why it is used and how to request access, correction or deletion.
